## 1. Overview & Canonical Lineages

BrahmaPath grounds digital behavioral regulation, cognitive resilience, and emotional poise in the primary texts of classical Indian psychology and medical science. Rather than utilizing external gamification or subjective heuristics, every metric, algorithm, and prompt is anchored in authoritative primary literature:

- **Upaniṣads**: *Kaṭha Upaniṣad* (Chariot psychometrics of consciousness) and *Taittirīya Upaniṣad* (Pañcakośa vital breath mechanics).
- **Śrīmad Bhagavad Gītā**: The psychological masterclass on cognitive discipline, detached performance (*Niṣkāma Karma*), the seven-stage cascade of desire (*Kāma-Krodha*), and sensory withdrawal (*Kūrma Pratyāhāra*).
- **Pātañjala Yoga Sūtras**: Classical cognitive science on impulse substitution (*Pratipakṣa Bhāvana*) and affective conditioning (*Citta Prasādanam*).
- **Āyurveda (Caraka Saṃhitā & Aṣṭāṅga Hṛdayam)**: The biological tripod of vitality (*Trayopastambha*), digestive fire (*Jaṭharāgni*), and biological humor dynamics (*Tridoṣa*).
- **Vedic Sūktas**: *Śukla Yajurveda* (Śiva-Saṅkalpa Sūktam for resolute mental orientation).

### Shloka 1: Bhagavad Gītā 2.41 — The Single-Pointed Intellect
* **Focus Area**: Attentional Sovereignty & Resolute Sankalpa
* **Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)**:
  ```text
  व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिरेकेह कुरुनन्दन ।
  बहुशाखा ह्यनन्ताश्च बुद्धयोऽव्यवसायिनाम् ॥
  ```
* **IAST Transliteration**:
  > *vyavasāyātmikā buddhir ekeha kuru-nandana |*
  > *bahu-śākhā hy anantāś ca buddhayo 'vyavasāyinām ||*
* **English Translation**:
  > "O descendant of Kuru, the intellect of those who are resolute on this path is single-pointed and unified, whereas the intellects of the irresolute are many-branched and endlessly divided."
* **Classical Commentary**:
  > *Adi Shankaracharya Bhashya*: Vyavasāyātmikā refers to the firm, unwavering conviction born of right discrimination (Pramāṇa), which destroys doubt. The unsteady intellect splits into infinite branches due to endless desires and scattered attachments.
* **Modern Application in BrahmaPath**:
  > Anchors the **Sacred Intention Space**. When digital multitasking fragments attention across dozens of tabs, apps, and impulses, cognitive energy collapses. Cultivating *vyavasāyātmikā buddhi* guides you to choose one primary mission and eliminate secondary noise.

### Shloka 3: Bhagavad Gītā 5.10 — The Lotus Leaf in Muddy Waters
* **Focus Area**: Performance Anxiety & Detached Action (Niṣkāma Karma)
* **Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)**:
  ```text
  ब्रह्मण्याधाय कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा करोति यः ।
  ```

* **Modern Application in BrahmaPath**:

* **Focus Area**: Mind-Wandering & Cognitive Steering
* **Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)**:

* **Modern Application in BrahmaPath**:
  > Re-educates users on the true definition of focus. Real mental discipline is the quiet, repeated act of noticing distraction and gently bringing attention back without self-judgment.

### Shloka 12: Bhagavad Gītā 18.37 — The Poison That Becomes Nectar
* **Focus Area**: Delayed Gratification & Enduring Discipline
* **Sanskrit (Devanāgarī)**:
  ```text
  यत्तदग्रे विषमिव परिणामेऽमृतोपमम् ।
  तत्सुखं सात्त्विकं प्रोक्तमात्मबुद्धिप्रसादजम् ॥
  ```
* **IAST Transliteration**:
  > *yat tad agre viṣam iva pariṇāme 'mṛtopamam |*
  > *tat sukhaṁ sāttvikaṁ proktam ātma-buddhi-prasāda-jam ||*
* **English Translation**:
  > "That which in the beginning feels like poison, but in the end transforms into nectar—that happiness is declared to be Sāttvika, born of the serene clarity of one's own intellect."
* **Classical Commentary**:
  > *Srila Prabhupada & Swami Mukundananda*: True self-discipline—waking early, sensory restraint, and deep intellectual focus—feels bitter like poison (*agre viṣam iva*) in the initial moments, but yields enduring nectar (*amṛtopamam*). Cheap sensory pleasures operate in exact reverse: sweet in the first few minutes, followed by exhaustion and regret.
* **Modern Application in BrahmaPath**:
  > Encourages seekers and sentinels to smile through the initial friction of waking early, exercising, or resisting cheap screen stimulation, knowing that lasting joy is born on the other side.
