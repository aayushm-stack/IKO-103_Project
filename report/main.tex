\section{Introduction, Motivation, and Target Audience}

\subsection{The Universal Human Need}
The challenge of directing attention, moderating biological rhythms, and preserving emotional equilibrium in the presence of recurring internal agitation is universal across human vocations. Whether an undergraduate student struggles against compulsive algorithmic social media feeds during late-night exam revision, a software engineer manages cognitive exhaustion across back-to-back corporate sprints, a retired elder seeks peaceful afternoon rhythm, or an infantry sentinel endures sub-zero vigilance at $18{,}000$ feet on the Siachen glacier, the underlying neuro-cognitive architecture is identical. The senses react impulsively to immediate sensory objects (\textit{Preyas}), hijacking conscious cognitive discernment (\textit{Buddhi}) and fracturing inner composure. \AppName\ is engineered for \textbf{any person seeking greater inner peace, a calm personal life outside of work, or sustained focus and vitality in daily duty} by translating profound, centuries-tested Indian philosophical frameworks into structured daily behavioral habits.

\subsection{The Dual Pathology of Existing Solutions}
Contemporary software solutions intended to support mental focus and stress regulation divide into two failed paradigms:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Punitive Western Habit Trackers and Streak Blockers:} Modern focus applications (e.g., rigid timers, website blockers, and binary day-counter streak apps) rely on coercive, extrinsic suppression. As demonstrated by human-computer interaction (HCI) literature~\cite{lyngs2019selfcontrol}, rigid barriers fail immediately once deactivated because they target automatic impulses without cultivating intrinsic discriminative mastery. Crucially, when an inevitable disruption occurs---such as a military night deployment, family emergency, or brief digital distraction---binary streak systems drop the counter to zero. This triggers deep psychological guilt, distress (\textit{Vi\d{s}\=ada}), and the clinically recognized \textit{Abstinence Violation Effect}~\cite{lyngs2019selfcontrol}, causing over $70\%$ of users to abandon the intervention entirely.
    \item \textbf{Passive Scripture Dictionaries and Chatbot Wrappers:} Existing scriptural wellness platforms (e.g., \textit{Situational Gita}, \textit{Living Geeta}, \textit{Ask Krishna Ji}) function as static verse repositories or superficial prompt wrappers around commercial Large Language Models (LLMs). When a user experiences acute stress or digital cravings, reading an abstract verse or engaging in an ungrounded chat produces fleeting intellectual agreement without altering somatic state, circadian rest, or physical impulse pathways. Furthermore, cloud-dependent LLM chatbots introduce unacceptable risks of theological hallucination, lack clinical crisis safeguards, and violate military Operational Security (OPSEC) by streaming sensitive personnel data to remote corporate servers.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Literature Review, Prior Art, and Systematic Derivation}

To establish rigorous scientific grounding, the architecture of \AppName\ was systematically derived from four research domains: human-computer interaction, military psychiatry, clinical psychometrics, and digital health patent jurisprudence.

\subsection{Dual-Systems Theory and Cognitive Friction in HCI}
In their comprehensive survey of 367 digital self-control tools, Lyngs et al.~\cite{lyngs2019selfcontrol} applied Kahneman's Dual-Systems Theory to demonstrate that external blockers only suppress automatic System~1 processes. Once the timer expires, the underlying compulsive loop reactivates with increased intensity. To achieve enduring self-regulation, digital interventions must introduce \textit{design friction} that slows impulsive execution and engages conscious, reflective System~2 cognitive deliberation. This empirical finding directly shaped our \textbf{Impulse Interceptor Engine}, which replaces binary lockouts with a seven-stage cognitive deceleration gateway and an active \textit{Vitarka-Pratipaksha} reframing protocol.

\subsection{High-Altitude Military Physiology and Homefront Distress}
Deployments in extreme high-altitude areas (EHAA, $15{,}000$--$20{,}000+$~ft) introduce severe physiological and psychological stressors. In a definitive clinical study of 334 soldiers deployed in EHAA, Rajesh, Bhat, Srivastava, and Das~\cite{rajesh2021extremealtitude} proved that environmental hypoxia induces high rates of anorexia, somatic nausea, and severely fragmented sleep architecture. Statistically, married service personnel exhibited significantly higher psychological morbidity on the General Health Questionnaire (\textit{GHQ-12}) compared to unmarried peers ($p < 0.01$). This vulnerability is driven not by battlefield fear, but by acute helplessness over domestic disputes and family crises occurring thousands of kilometers away while isolated in sub-zero fiberglass huts. This clinical evidence directly generated our \textbf{High-Altitude Terrain Engine}, the \textbf{Ja\d{t}har\=agni Ration \& Hydration Check} in `SentinelSadhana.tsx` (which triggers warm-water and soup micro-prompts when nausea suppresses appetite), and the \textbf{Homefront Reassurance Bridge} in `SentinelCompass.tsx`.

\subsection{The ``Last Straw'' Phenomenon and Border Fatigue}
Investigating non-combat operational attrition across the Armed Forces, Srivastava et al.~\cite{srivastava2011stressarmedforces} and longitudinal BSF paramilitary studies by Chhabra \& Chhabra~\cite{chhabra2013bsfstress} demonstrated that fratricidal surges and stress breakdowns rarely originate from isolated catastrophic incidents. Rather, months of cumulative circadian sleep debt ($36.3\%$), irregular mess timings and heavy sentry-tea dependence ($36.0\%$), and suppressed hierarchy resentment progressively deplete the soldier's inner coping capacity. When this reserve is exhausted, an innocuous remark from a peer or a minor domestic argument acts as the catastrophic \textit{``last straw.''} In response, \AppName\ engineered the \textbf{Inner Coping \& Patience Reserve ($Ojas$ Tank 1--5 slider)}, the \textbf{Watch-Tea Stimulant Profile Check}, and the \textbf{10-Minute $V\bar{a}\dot{n}$-maya Tapas Speech Shield} (BG 17.15) to break the escalation loop before speech occurs.

\subsection{Military Family Resilience and Peer Cohesion}
Studies by Chatterjee et al.~\cite{chatterjee2024resilience}, Pawar et al.~\cite{pawar2012occupationalstress}, and the Armed Forces Medical Services Concept on Holistic Fitness~\cite{afms2023holisticfitness} confirm that peer camaraderie (\textit{Sahyog}) and structured family communication are the primary psychological buffers protecting armed service personnel. When institutional mental health tools offer cold, individualistic advice (e.g., ``focus only on your mission and detach from home''), personnel reject them as unfeeling. \AppName\ replaces clinical detachment with warm \textbf{Sahyogi (Battle-Buddy) Psychology}, integrating a pre-scripted 2-Sentence Homefront Reassurance script, pre-call de-armoring exercises, and peer debrief telemetry.

\subsection{Clinical IKS Validation and Tri-Guna Psychometrics}
The application of ancient Indian psychological concepts to contemporary cognitive psychotherapy is clinically established. M. S. Reddy~\cite{reddy2012psychotherapy} systematically mapped the dialogue between Krishna and Arjuna in the \textit{Bhagavad Gita} to modern crisis Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), identifying verses 6.26, 2.14, 12.13, 2.56, and 6.5 as precise cognitive reframing interventions for acute anxiety, grief, and emotional paralysis. \section{Core Mathematical Models and Psychometric State Machine}

The computational core of \AppName\ consists of five interconnected mathematical engines, operating entirely on-device without cloud network round-trips.

\subsection{Engine 1: Trayopastambha Yukta Score Engine}
Rooted in \textit{Charaka Samhita} (Sutrasthana 11.35) and \textit{Bhagavad Gita} 6.17, the Yukta Engine evaluates physiological and attentional equilibrium ($Y \in [0, 100]$) across the three biological sub-pillars: \textit{\=Ah\=ara} (nourishment and sensory intake), \textit{Nidr\=a} (circadian sleep recovery), and \textit{Brahmacharya} (vital energy conservation and deep focus):
\begin{equation}
    Y = S_{\text{\=Ah\=ara}} + S_{\text{Nidr\=a}} + S_{\text{Brahmacharya}}
\end{equation}
where $S_{\text{\=Ah\=ara}} \in [0, 34]$, $S_{\text{Nidr\=a}} \in [0, 33]$, and $S_{\text{Brahmacharya}} \in [0, 33]$. If an individual pillar has not yet been logged today by the user, its score defaults strictly to $0$ via the \texttt{loggedPillars} tracking interface.

\subsubsection{\=Ah\=ara Pillar Formulation (Max 34 Points)}
\begin{equation}
    S_{\text{\=Ah\=ara}} = \min(34,\; P_{\text{nutrition}} + P_{\text{screen}} + P_{\text{mindful}})
\end{equation}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Nutrition / Ration Score ($P_{\text{nutrition}} \in \{2, 6, 10\}$):} For Stage~1 civilian users, fresh Sattvic intake awards $10$, Rajasic stimulant meals award $6$, and stale/junk Tamasic food awards $2$. For Stage~2 defense sentries, a balanced operational ration awards $10$, high watch-caffeine tea awards $6$, and irregular/missed rations award $2$.
    \item \textbf{Self-Anchored Relative Screen Time ($P_{\text{screen}} \in [0, 12]$):} Rather than applying arbitrary population cutoffs (e.g., ``2 hours per day''), the engine anchors against the user's personal baseline $M_{\text{baseline}} = \max(60, \text{\texttt{baselineMinutesAtLog}})$, computing the relative ratio $r = \frac{M_{\text{digital}}}{M_{\text{baseline}}}$. The score is awarded via a smooth piecewise-linear curve:
    \begin{equation}
        P_{\text{screen}}(r) = \begin{cases}
            \text{round}(12 - 2.67r), & r \le 0.75 \quad (\text{Zone 1: Significant Drop, } 12 \rightarrow 10) \\
            \text{round}(10 - 12(r - 0.75)), & 0.75 < r \le 1.00 \quad (\text{Dropped/Maintained, } 10 \rightarrow 7) \\
            \max(0, \text{round}(7 - 11.67(r - 1.00))), & r > 1.00 \quad (\text{Zone 4: Above Average Taper, } 7 \rightarrow 0)
        \end{cases}
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Sensory Mindfulness / Call Digestion ($P_{\text{mindful}} \in [0, 12]$):} Computed continuously from the 1--5 interface slider $x$:
    \begin{equation}
        P_{\text{mindful}}(x) = \max(0, \min(12, \text{round}((x - 1) \times 3))) \in \{0, 3, 6, 9, 12\}
    \end{equation}
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Nidr\=a Pillar Formulation (Max 33 Points)}

\subsection{Engine 2: Impulse Interceptor PRQ Engine}
Grounded in \textit{Bhagavad Gita} 2.62--63 and \textit{Yoga Sutra} 2.33, the Pratipaksha Resilience Quotient ($P \in [0, 100]$) measures real-time impulse deceleration. Let $E_{\text{today}} = E_{\text{int}} \cup E_{\text{slip}}$ denote today's recorded urge events, and define the activity indicator $\delta_{\text{int}} = \mathbb{I}(|E_{\text{today}}| > 0)$.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Standby Rule:} If $\delta_{\text{int}} = 0$, the user recorded no urges or slips today. $P = 0$, and the module is marked as Standby (excluded from Chariot Composure weighting).
    \item \textbf{Active Slip Penalization:} If $\delta_{\text{int}} = 1$ and $|E_{\text{int}}| = 0$ (the user only logged Honest Slips today), $P = 0$ and \textbf{is actively counted}, directly pulling down the composite Chariot Composure.
    \item \textbf{Intensity-Weighted Dynamic Ratio:} When $|E_{\text{int}}| > 0$, each event is weighted by its recorded intensity slider $I \in \{1..5\}$:
    \begin{align}
        W_{+} &= \sum_{i \in E_{\text{int}}} (0.7 + 0.3 I_i) \\
        W_{-} &= \sum_{j \in E_{\text{slip}}} (0.8 + 0.35 I_j) \\
        R_{\text{raw}} &= \frac{W_{+}}{W_{+} + W_{-}} \\
        F_{\text{intensity}} &= \min\left(1.0,\; 0.78 + 0.045 \sum_{i \in E_{\text{int}}} I_i\right) \\
        P &= \max(0, \min(100, \text{round}(100 \times R_{\text{raw}} \times F_{\text{intensity}})))
    \end{align}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Engine 3: Bh\=ava Compass Emotional Conditioning Engine}
Grounded in \textit{Patanjali Yoga Sutra} 1.33 (\textit{Chitta Pras\=adanam}), the Bh\=ava Engine assesses affective equilibrium ($B \in [0, 100]$). In contrast to older models that defaulted to an arbitrary 75, \AppName\ anchors the neutral baseline at exactly $50$:
\begin{equation}
    B = \max\Big(0,\; \min\Big(100,\; 50 + 10 N_{\text{pos}} - 12 N_{\text{unresolved}} + 4 N_{\text{resolved}}\Big)\Big)
\end{equation}
where $N_{\text{pos}} \in \{0..3\}$ is the count of positive psychological buffers (capped at $+30$), $N_{\text{unresolved}}$ is the count of unaddressed interpersonal frictions (penalized at $-12$ each), and $N_{\text{resolved}}$ is the count of successfully resolved negative frictions (rewarded at $+4$ each, reflecting emotional resolution). The theoretical ceiling of $100$ is earned when $N_{\text{pos}} = 3$ and $N_{\text{resolved}} = 5$ ($50 + 30 + 20 = 100$). The activity indicator is $\delta_{\text{bhava}} = \mathbb{I}(N_{\text{pos}} + N_{\text{unresolved}} + N_{\text{resolved}} > 0)$.

\subsection{Engine 4: Master Chariot Composure Index and Multi-Gate State Transitions}
Drawing upon the chariot allegory of the \textit{Katha Upanishad} (1.3.3--4), where the Self is the Rider, the Intellect (\textit{Buddhi}) is the Charioteer, the Mind (\textit{Manas}) is the Reins, and the Senses (\textit{Indriyas}) are the Horses, the Master Composure Index ($C \in [0, 100]$) dynamically synthesizes physical, impulse, and emotional telemetry:

\subsubsection{Dynamic Branch Weighting}
\begin{equation}
    C = \begin{cases}
        0, & \text{if } k = 0 \land \delta_{\text{int}} = 0 \land \delta_{\text{bhava}} = 0 \quad (\text{Dawn Inactivity}) \\
        \text{round}(0.65 Y + 0.35 B), & \text{if } \delta_{\text{int}} = 0 \quad (\text{Interceptor on Standby}) \\
        \text{round}(0.50 Y + 0.25 P + 0.25 B), & \text{if } \delta_{\text{int}} = 1 \quad (\text{Interceptor Active})
    \end{cases}
\end{equation}
where $k \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ denotes the number of distinct Sadhana pillars explicitly logged today.

\subsubsection{Earned Multi-Gate State Thresholds}
To prevent artificial score inflation (such as logging a single morning pillar turning the entire interface green), the Chariot State transitions through four earned gates:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Resting (Slate Grey Artwork):} Triggered when the user has logged zero activity across all modules today ($k = 0 \land \delta_{\text{int}} = 0 \land \delta_{\text{bhava}} = 0$), or when severe Tamasic depletion occurs ($\text{Guna} = \text{Tamas} \land C < 42$).
    \item \textbf{Galloping (Red/Terracotta Warning):} Triggered when $C < 50$, OR when $k = 1 \land C < 58$. This strict conditional barrier guarantees that logging one incomplete pillar cannot falsely jump the Chariot to Brisk or Calm.
    \item \textbf{Brisk (Amber/Orange Dynamic Steering):} Triggered when $C \ge 50$ and the state does not meet all three simultaneous criteria for Calm.

\section{Native Android OS Notification Engine and Full-Stack Architecture}

\subsection{Root-Cause Analysis of Capacitor Repetition Scheduling Failure}
During hardware Android deployment, an acute scheduling bug was uncovered in Capacitor's native notification bridge (\texttt{LocalNotificationManager.java}). When developers configure a daily recurring alarm by combining \texttt{every: 'day'} with \texttt{on: \{ hour, minute \}}, the underlying Java implementation prioritizes the interval trigger over the match object:
\begin{equation}
    \text{if (trigger.getEvery() != null) } \longrightarrow \text{ignores } \text{trigger.getOn()}
\end{equation}
Consequently, rather than firing at 08:30 and 13:30, the Android OS fired all scheduled daily notifications simultaneously at the exact millisecond of app launch, corrupting the circadian reminder rhythm.

\subsection{7-Day Rolling Explicit Timestamp Queue Implementation}
\AppName\ resolved this native limitation by engineering a \textbf{7-Day Rolling Explicit Timestamp Queue}. Instead of relying on buggy recurring flags, the background engine calculates concrete calendar timestamps (\texttt{at: new Date(year, month, day, hour, min)}) across the next rolling 168 hours. Each notification is registered through Android's \texttt{AlarmManager} with \texttt{allowWhileIdle = true} on an Importance-5 channel, leveraging the native \texttt{USE\_EXACT\_ALARM} permission to bypass Android OS battery-saver Doze mode.

\subsection{Lockscreen Geometry and Notification Anchors}
To prevent Android's system UI from truncating notifications on standard mobile lockscreens, all notification copy was strictly engineered under \textbf{95 characters (2-line lockscreen ceiling)}. The system schedules seven daily prompts:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Four Fixed Anchors:} 08:30 Morning Sankalpa, 13:30 Midday Trayopastambha Pause, 18:30 Evening Sensory Reset, and 21:30 Night Rest Reflection.
    \item \textbf{Three Randomized Mindfulness Windows:} Jittered intervals (10:15--12:15 Deep Work Focus, 15:45--17:30 Emotional Equilibrium Check, and 20:00--21:15 Digital Curfew Warning) that counteract predictable user habituation.
\end{itemize}

\section{Discussion, Future Work, and Why BrahmaPath Deliberately Rejects an AI Chatbot}

\subsection{The Architectural Rejection of Large Language Model Chatbots}
A central design decision of \AppName\ is its explicit rejection of conversational LLM chatbot interfaces (e.g., ``Chat with Lord Krishna AI''). While conversational agents represent a prominent commercial trend in digital wellness, our architectural analysis revealed four decisive objections:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{The ``Why Not Just Use ChatGPT?'' Principle:} If a mental health or philosophical application is merely a prompt-engineered wrapper around an external cloud LLM, the product possesses zero technological defensibility and zero user retention value. A user encountering an obstacle will simply query ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini directly on their device. A true digital therapeutic must provide structured psychometric telemetry, closed-loop state tracking, and somatic interventions that an unanchored text box cannot replicate.
    \item \textbf{Theological Hallucination and Clinical Safety:} Commercial LLMs are probabilistic token predictors that frequently fabricate Sanskrit verses, conflate conflicting schools of Indian philosophy, or generate unsafe, invalidating advice during acute psychiatric distress. In contrast, every scriptural verse, transliteration, exegesis, and cognitive reframe in \AppName\ is 100\% deterministic, human-curated, and reviewed against classical \textit{Sankara Bhashya} commentaries.
    \item \textbf{100\% Offline Military OPSEC Compliance:} Frontline defense deployments occur in high-security, forward operational pickets (Siachen, LOC, LAC) characterized by zero commercial internet access, strict radio silence, and stringent OPSEC protocols. Cloud-based LLM APIs cannot function in disconnected environments and violate military data sovereignty by transmitting personnel emotional telemetry to third-party commercial clouds. \AppName\ operates strictly offline, storing zero data off-device.
    \item \textbf{Action (\textit{Sadhana}) Over Endless Intellectualizing:} Ancient Indian philosophy emphasizes that mental transformation arises from disciplined, continuous action (\textit{Abhy\=asa} and \textit{Kriy\=a}), not passive intellectual debate. Endless text conversations with a chatbot enable cognitive avoidance, allowing users to talk about their stress rather than putting the phone face down, regulating their breath, and restoring biological equilibrium.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Future Empirical Validation and Clinical Roadmap}
Future work is oriented toward three clinical and technological milestones:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Circadian Schedule Adaptation:} Expanding our six micro-environments with dynamic seasonal and schedule-aware adjustments (e.g., student examination weeks versus semester breaks; military pre-deployment, forward picket, and post-leave transition phases).
    \item \textbf{Regional Audio-Guided Pratyahara:} Integrating offline, native-language voice tracks (in Hindi, Punjabi, and regional Indian languages) for tactile \textit{K\=urma} eye-cupping and down-regulation breathing, tailored for exhausted sentries and senior citizens.
    \item \textbf{Formal Longitudinal Clinical Trials:} Conducting randomized controlled trials (RCTs) in collaboration with the \textbf{IKSMHA Centre at IIT Mandi}, measuring pre/post intervention shifts in the \textit{Mysore Triguna Scale}, the \textit{General Health Questionnaire (GHQ-12)}, and objective sleep latency across student and paramilitary cohorts.
\end{enumerate}
